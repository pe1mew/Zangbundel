Zangbundel – icon proposals

Common language: night-blue background (the app's night theme), fire orange and yellow, white figures, Bluetooth signal in light blue. All five are 512 × 512 vectors with the key content inside the central safe zone, so each can become the adaptive launcher icon and the 512 px Play icon.

01 · Boek en baken

An open songbook sends out a Bluetooth beacon: the rune stands on the book like a flame, signal arcs on both sides, one orange note on the page for the singing. Group is implied by the broadcast, not drawn.

96 px and 48 px, as on a home screen

File: design/icon-proposals/01-boek-en-baken.svg

02 · Kring van zangers

Five singers around an orange songbook that glows like a fire; dashed Bluetooth ripples ring the book; small notes rise from three singers. The most literal "group" of the five.

96 px and 48 px, as on a home screen

File: design/icon-proposals/02-kring-van-zangers.svg

03 · Vlammenboek

The pages of the songbook rise as a campfire flame with a note inside; three heads sit in front of the book; a Bluetooth signal fans out top right. Reads as "campfire" first.

96 px and 48 px, as on a home screen

File: design/icon-proposals/03-vlammenboek.svg

04 · Noot en golven

One large note whose flag is a two-page booklet; Bluetooth waves leave the note head and reach three smaller notes in fire colours, the singers answering. Most abstract.

96 px and 48 px, as on a home screen

File: design/icon-proposals/04-noot-en-golven.svg

05 · Embleem

A scouting-style badge: beacon rune and arcs at the top, three singers with rising notes, an orange songbook at the base, yellow ring. Most detailed; test at small sizes.

96 px and 48 px, as on a home screen
