# Sharing the app without internet – 2026-10-27

FR-DIST-05, FR-DIST-08; guiDesign D-84 (v0.52); architecture A-18 (v0.17); assessment SP-20. Debug build (about 20 MB; the release build is about 5 MB) on the A52, installed by adb as one APK; the Huawei as the second phone. **Result: the copy handed to the share sheet is the installed package byte for byte; Quick Share carried it to the S23, where the owner opened and installed it, and the package installed there is the same file. Bluetooth is not tested.**

**Not tested:** Bluetooth file transfer; Quick Share between phones on different accounts (the receiver then has to accept); a first install, rather than an update, on a phone without the app, including the *install from this source* prompt of step 3 (the S23 had allowed it before); a Play install (architecture Q1). The received file stays in the S23's `Download/Quick Share/` until its owner deletes it; the app cannot reach it.
