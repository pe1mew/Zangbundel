**The reception grid** (D-81) was open on both singers: the owner had opened it with a long press, as designed.

**Defect: the home screen's scan dies 60 s after a leader ends.** On the S23 the campfire of 17:49 was ended with *Afsluiten*, then *Terug naar start*; the home screen's discovery scan started at 17:50:11 and stopped at 17:50:15, and no scan of the app followed until the screen was left (`dumpsys bluetooth_manager`), while the screen kept showing *Zoeken naar kampvuren…*. At 18:33 the A52's campfire was therefore not listed. Cause: `Gathering.end()` lets the radio linger 60 s (`LeaderSession.END_LINGER_MS`) and only then cancels the session, whose listening loop for other campfires calls `stopScan()` in its `finally`; the transport has one scan callback, so that call stops the home screen's scan. Workaround in 0.1.0: leave the home screen and come back. **Fixed** the same evening: `end()` cancels that loop at once, and its `finally` stops the scan only during a listen. Checked with a debug build of the fix on the S23: it led *kat-bos* for 35 s (listens 18:46:13 and 18:46:44, 2 s each), ended at 18:47:09, *Terug naar start* at 18:47:14; its home-screen scan started at 18:47:15.846 and was still running at 18:48:48, past 18:48:09 where the old code stopped it, and at 18:48:34 the list showed the A52's new campfire *mees-gong · Dichtbij*. `./gradlew assembleDebug check` passed. The Play build 0.1.0 keeps the defect until the next upload.
