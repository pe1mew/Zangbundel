# Resume after a crash – 2026-10-27

guiDesign D-67 as built in v0.39, BLE protocol 10.1 step 2 and 10.9 (v0.39), architecture 5.4 (v0.13), FR-GATH-13. The A52 led a campfire with the setlist *Voorbeeldavond*; the S23 and the Huawei sang. The leader's app was killed with `am force-stop`, which ends the process without any of the app's own clean-up, as a crash or the system would. Driven over adb. **Result: the resume screen came up, *Hervatten* brought the gathering back as it was, and both singers carried on without a new registration; no crash on any phone.**
