# D-03, D-05, D-07: the first campfire in the app – 2026-10-26

The first run of a gathering through the app's own screens instead of the two-phone test: started from the home screen with a prepared setlist, joined from the list of campfires nearby, three real songs pushed with *Nu*, ended with *Afsluiten*. **Result: D-03 passes; D-05 passes for its delivery but its time criterion is open (NFR-PERF-01); D-07 failed in the morning and passed in the afternoon, after the leave notice was made reliable and the gathering memory built.** Two app bugs and one resource leak were found and fixed during the run; a stuck Bluetooth stack had to be cleared by switching Bluetooth off and on.

## Found and fixed

1. **The singer went deaf right after joining.** The home screen's cleanup stopped "the discovery scan" when the singer's page opened, but by then the scan belonged to the singer engine: the singer heard nothing after selecting the gathering and showed *opnieuw verbinden…* 10 s later. `Gathering.stopScanning` now leaves the radio alone while a gathering runs.
2. **The singer's page stayed on the previous song.** It moved on the beacon's new `seq`, which arrives before the state that names the song. It now moves on the verified state, and a song arriving later becomes current when the latest state names it.
3. **A cancelled GATT connect leaked its client.** `AndroidGatt.open` did not close the `BluetoothGatt` when its caller's timeout cancelled it (the 2 s of a leave, ble-protocol 10.11), leaving a pending connection and one of Android's client slots behind. It now closes it.

Two observations to keep: the leader's memory counted 5 singers, because three earlier leaves that did not reach it left stale registrations until the 10-minute rule of 10.9; and it lists *De uil zat in de olmen*, which the singer never got and which this record cannot account for (a stray test tap on its *Nu* is likely). Pushes are logged from now on (index only, A-20). After many joins and leaves in a short time the Huawei once stopped finding the campfire until its scan was restarted after a pause: Android and EMUI limit how often an app may start a scan, and every join and leave restarts it. A scan that runs on from the home screen into the gathering and back would avoid this; not built.

## Not passed, open

- **D-07:** passed in the afternoon (above): the count drops, and the singer holds a memory and no lyrics.
- **D-05, time:** 4.9–5.8 s against the 5 s of NFR-PERF-01 (FRD Q14), as in run 18.
- **A stuck Bluetooth stack:** from about 10:21 every GATT connection between the two phones stalled at the MTU exchange (the A52's ATT server stopped answering after the first discovery request), also in the two-phone test, which had worked on 2026-10-25. Switching Bluetooth off and on on both phones cleared it. Cause unknown; worth watching in field tests, since a leader cannot see it.
