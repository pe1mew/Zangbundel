## Full description (≤ 4000)

Zangbundel ("songbook" in Dutch) is the songbook app for the campfire, for example at scouting events.

One phone is the leader. The leader starts a gathering and picks the songs; the app sends the lyrics over Bluetooth to every phone around the fire, at the same moment and without internet, Wi-Fi or a hotspot. Everyone sees the same words when the song starts.

How it works
• The leader starts a gathering and shows a QR code, or singers pick the gathering from a list.
• Singers scan the code and join. No account, no registration.
• The leader picks a song; the lyrics appear on everyone's phone.
• Singers scroll along line by line, large and clear: black on white by day, white on black at night.
• Singers can send a song request to the leader; the leader or a helper decides what is sung.
• Anyone can leave at any time. The leader ends the gathering; the lyrics disappear from the phones again and the list of songs sung stays as a memory.

Song libraries
The app contains no lyrics of its own. Songs come from libraries that a group or association maintains, with their licence information. You download a library when you have internet, for example at home before camp; after that no internet is needed. Singers without the library receive the words from the leader over Bluetooth.

Privacy
Zangbundel collects nothing and sends nothing to the developer. No account, no ads, no analytics. Your display name and your song requests are encrypted to the leader's phone; lyrics are broadcast unencrypted because they contain nothing personal. Everything you receive during a gathering is deleted when it ends.

Permissions
• Bluetooth: to find and join gatherings, and to broadcast as leader.
• On Android 11 and older, Android requires the location permission to scan for Bluetooth devices. Zangbundel never reads your location.
• Internet: only to download song libraries, and only when you confirm.

Languages: Dutch and English; song libraries can be in any language.

Zangbundel is open source: https://github.com/pe1mew/Zangbundel
